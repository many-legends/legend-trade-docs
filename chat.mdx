Chat is built into Legend inside clans, in group chats, and between traders on the platform.

- **Clan chat** -- persistent group chat for your clan. Share calls and build together.
- **Direct messages** -- start a private conversation with another trader. Blocking and recipient privacy settings apply; a message may arrive as a request until it is accepted.
- **Group chats** -- private group conversations. Invitations can also require approval based on each recipient's privacy settings.

Chat is where Legend comes alive. Share trades, discuss markets, and stay connected to the people around you.
