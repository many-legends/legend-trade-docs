Legend Capital lets you complete a one-step trading evaluation and unlock a funded account. Evaluation accounts use paper capital; funded accounts remain subject to the program rules shown at checkout and in your account dashboard.

## Programs

| Program | Funded Capital | One-Time Fee |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Rookie | $5,000 | $55 |
| Prime | $10,000 | $110 |
| Legend | $25,000 | $250 |

Every current program has the same evaluation rules:

- 10% profit target
- 4% maximum daily loss
- 7% maximum drawdown
- Up to 5x leverage
- No time limit

<Note>
  Review the confirmation screen before paying. It is the authoritative statement of the account size, fee, loss limits, profit target, and profit split for your purchase.
</Note>

## Evaluation

Your evaluation begins with the program's stated paper balance. Reach the profit target without breaching either loss limit to pass. The account dashboard shows your current equity, daily-loss floor, maximum-drawdown floor, and progress toward the target.

The maximum drawdown is trailing: as the account reaches a new high-water mark, the drawdown floor can move up with it. The daily-loss limit resets on the platform's daily schedule.

## Funded Account

After passing, Legend creates a funded account at the selected program size. The funded account keeps the program's risk limits. Eligible profits can be requested from the Capital dashboard, where the available amount, profit split, payout status, and history are shown.

Payout availability is based on profit above the starting balance and is subject to a rolling 24-hour platform cap. An account with a payout already in progress cannot submit another request until it resolves.

## Transparency

The [Capital transparency dashboard](https://app.legend.trade/capital/transparency) reports aggregate program revenue, payouts, pass rates, funded traders, performance, funding statistics, and recent activity.
