Clans are Legend's team system -- compete together, climb together.

Clans unlock fee rewards for everyone based on the clan's rolling 30-day Legend trading volume. Members receive a rebate on their own builder fees, while leaders receive the same rate as a share of other members' builder fees.

| Rolling 30-Day Clan Volume | Member Rebate | Leader Share |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Under \$100,000 | 0% | 0% |
| \$100,000+ | 1% | 1% |
| \$500,000+ | 2% | 2% |
| \$1,000,000+ | 3% | 3% |
| \$5,000,000+ | 4% | 4% |
| \$10,000,000+ | 5% | 5% |

Only current members' mainnet notional traded through Legend while they are in the clan counts. Entries and exits both contribute. When someone leaves, their volume no longer contributes to the clan's tier.

- To create a clan, you must have at least $1,000 in lifetime notional trading volume on Legend
- Capped at 300 members -- you can only be in one clan at a time
- Every clan has its own ELO, tracked separately from individual rank
- Launch clan wars against rivals and climb the seasonal clan leaderboard
- Each clan has its own chat, member list, and shared stats
- Invite friends or recruit from the leaderboard

Leaders receive the member rebate on their own trades, but they do not receive a leader share from themselves. Fees are charged normally, then earned rebates and leader shares accrue as claimable rewards.

Your clan is your identity on Legend -- who you ride with.
