Activity shows trading behavior across Legend. It is separate from the [Feed](/feed), which contains posts, chart notes, replies, and market recaps.

## Trending Positions

The top of Activity highlights positions drawing attention across the platform.

- **Trending Positions** shows currently active positions
- **Greatest Hits** shows notable positions from the all-time view

Select a position to inspect its direction, performance, trader, and any attached notes, then jump to the relevant market.

## Live Trades

The trade stream shows recent entries, increases, reductions, and closes. Use the scope menu to choose which activity appears:

- **All** -- trades from across Legend
- **Following** -- trades from people you follow
- **Clan** -- trades from members of your clan

Select a trade to view its details and open the corresponding market.
