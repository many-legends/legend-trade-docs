---

The Feed is where traders publish market ideas and discuss them. It is separate from [Activity](/activity), which shows trending positions and live trades.

Feed items can include:

- Posts and chart notes from traders
- Trading context attached to a position or symbol
- Replies and conversations
- Legend's daily market recap

Use the **All**, **Following**, and **Clan** filters to choose whose posts you see. Selecting a chart note opens its conversation alongside the relevant market chart when a symbol is attached.
