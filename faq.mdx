## General

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is the difference between Legend and Hyperliquid?">
    Legend is a competitive social trading platform built on top of Hyperliquid. Hyperliquid is the underlying exchange -- it provides the liquidity, order matching, and on-chain settlement. Legend adds trader profiles, ELO rankings, clans, copy trading, chat, and a social feed.

    TLDR: Hyperliquid is the engine. Legend is the experience built on top of it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What can I trade on Legend?">
    You can trade perpetual contracts (perps) on all assets available on Hyperliquid. This includes major crypto assets, memecoins, and more as Hyperliquid continues to add listings.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I sign up?">
    You can sign up with **Google**, **X (Twitter)**, **Telegram**, **email**, a **passkey**, or by connecting an existing wallet. Legend uses Privy to create a non-custodial embedded wallet for you automatically.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is Legend available on mobile?">
    Yes. Legend is available on iOS via the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/legend-trade-anything/id6754900991), on Android via [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=trade.legend.legend), and in any mobile browser at [app.legend.trade](https://app.legend.trade).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I create trading subaccounts?">
    User-managed trading subaccounts are not currently supported. Connected login and social accounts are managed separately in Settings.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Deposits & Withdrawals

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I deposit funds?">
    Select **Deposit** in Legend to open the Unifold funding flow. Unifold presents the funding methods available for your location and routes the deposit to your Hyperliquid address on HyperCore.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My deposit is not showing up. What should I do?">
    Wait 5-10 minutes for the transaction to confirm and bridge. If it still hasn't arrived, verify that you deposited a supported token on a supported network. If the issue persists, reach out to us on [X (Twitter)](https://x.com/legendtrade) for help.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I withdraw?">
    Withdrawals are processed via Unifold. Initiate a withdrawal from the Legend app and choose your destination chain and currency -- Unifold supports withdrawing to many chains and currencies for maximum convenience.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My withdrawal is stuck. What should I do?">
    Withdrawals typically complete within a few minutes. If yours appears stuck, message us on [X (Twitter)](https://x.com/legendtrade) with your wallet address and we'll help resolve it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Referrals & Rewards

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do referrals work?">
    Every Legend user gets a unique referral link. When someone signs up through your link and trades, you earn a percentage of the builder fee revenue from their trades. Find your referral link in your profile.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the referral rate?">
    The base referral rate is **10%** of builder fee revenue. Your rolling 30-day trading volume on Legend unlocks higher rates:

    | Rolling 30-Day Volume | Referral Rate |
    |-----------------------|---------------|
    | Base                  | 10%           |
    | $100,000+             | 12.5%         |
    | $1,000,000+           | 15%           |
    | $10,000,000+          | 17.5%         |
    | $100,000,000+         | 20%           |

    Tiers are recalculated automatically from executed mainnet notional traded through Legend during the trailing 30 days. Deposits, withdrawals, transfers, paper trades, and trades made through another app do not count. Your tier may decrease as older trades leave the rolling window.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do shared chart notes count as referrals?">
    Yes. When you share a chart note or feed item externally, anyone who signs up through that link is automatically counted as your referral.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do clan fee rewards work?">
    A clan's rolling 30-day eligible Legend volume unlocks a shared rate for members and leaders:

    | Rolling 30-Day Clan Volume | Member Rebate | Leader Share |
    |----------------------------|---------------|--------------|
    | Under $100,000             | 0%            | 0%           |
    | $100,000+                  | 1%            | 1%           |
    | $500,000+                  | 2%            | 2%           |
    | $1,000,000+                | 3%            | 3%           |
    | $5,000,000+                | 4%            | 4%           |
    | $10,000,000+               | 5%            | 5%           |

    Members receive the rate as a rebate on their own builder fees. Leaders receive the same rate from other members' builder fees and also receive the member rebate on their own trades. Only current members' mainnet volume traded through Legend while in the clan counts. Fees are charged normally, and rewards are claimed from your profile.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Social

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What are Clans?">
    Clans are Legend's team system. Trade together, grow together. Each clan has its own chat, member list, shared stats, and PnL-based leaderboard standing. You can only be in one clan at a time, and clans are capped at 300 members. Rolling 30-day clan volume unlocks matching 0–5% member rebates and leader shares.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I create a Clan?">
    To create a clan, you must have at least **$1,000** in lifetime notional trading volume on Legend. Once created, you can invite friends or recruit from the leaderboard.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is copy-trading?">
    Copy trading lets you mirror another trader's positions. You choose how much to allocate, and the system proportionally reflects the target trader's position changes in a dedicated copy wallet. Copy trading is available from trader profiles.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What chat features are available?">
    - **Clan chat** -- persistent group chat for your clan
    - **Direct messages** -- private trader conversations, subject to blocking and recipient privacy settings
    - **Group chats** -- private group conversations; some invitations arrive as requests that must be accepted
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What appears on the feed?">
    The Feed contains trader posts, chart notes, replies, and Legend's daily market recap. Use the All, Following, and Clan filters to choose whose posts you see. Live trades and trending positions appear separately under Activity.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Fees

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What are Legend's trading fees?">
    Your total fee per trade has two parts: the Hyperliquid trading fee (volume-tiered maker/taker rates based on your rolling 14-day volume) and the Legend platform fee. Maker rebates are paid out on each fill directly to your wallet. For full details, see the [Fees page](/about/fees).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the Legend platform fee?">
    A platform fee (builder code fee) is charged on top of Hyperliquid trading fees: **0.05%** for standard perpetual trades and **0.10%** for copy-wallet perpetual trades. A portion of this fee funds referral payouts, clan member rebates, and clan leader shares. Copy-trading profit share is separate from these trading fees.
  </Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
