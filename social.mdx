Social connects everything on the platform: who you follow, whose ideas you read, and who you trade alongside.

Your profile and performance are public, posts let you share market context, and live activity shows how traders are positioned.

- **Feed** -- read posts, chart notes, conversations, and daily recaps
- **Activity** -- follow trending positions and live trades across the platform
- **Clans** -- team up, share progress, and unlock rewards together
- **Following** -- track the traders you care about
- **Chat** -- talk to clanmates and friends directly on the platform
