Social connects everything on the platform: who you follow, who you watch, and who you trade alongside.

Your trading activity can live on the feed, your reputation is public, and your community grows as you perform.

- **Feed** -- see what traders around you are doing in real-time
- **Clans** -- team up, share progress, and unlock rewards together
- **Following** -- track the traders you care about
- **Chat** -- talk to clanmates and friends directly on the platform
