Legend trades are executed on [Hyperliquid](https://hyperliquid.xyz). Your total fee on each trade has two parts: the **Hyperliquid trading fee** and the **Legend platform fee**.

## Hyperliquid Trading Fees

Hyperliquid charges maker/taker fees based on your rolling 14-day weighted volume and HYPE staking tier. Perpetual and spot volume share one volume tier, with spot volume weighted double. Sub-account volume counts toward the master account, and all sub-accounts share the same tier.

Hyperliquid controls this external fee schedule and may change it independently of Legend. See [Hyperliquid's current fee documentation](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/trading/fees) for live perpetual, spot, staking-discount, and maker-rebate rates.

## Legend Platform Fee

A platform fee (builder code fee) is charged on every trade placed through Legend, on top of the Hyperliquid trading fee:

| Market | Platform Fee |
| --- | --- |
| Perpetuals | 0.05% |
| Copy-trading perpetuals | 0.10% |
| Spot | 0.25% |

A portion of this fee funds [referral payouts, clan member rebates, and clan leader shares](/rewards-and-points/referrals-and-rewards).

Copy-wallet orders use a separate 0.10% Legend builder fee because they are executed by the copy-trading service. This is separate from any profit-share rate set by the trader you copy.

Clan fee rewards do not reduce the fee charged when a trade executes. Eligible member rebates and leader shares accrue separately as claimable rewards.

## Total Fee

Your total fee per trade is the sum of both components:

**Total fee** = Hyperliquid trading fee + Legend platform fee

Your actual Hyperliquid component depends on your current volume and staking tier. The order interface displays the applicable fee before submission.

## Referred User Cashback

Users who signed up through a referral link earn **10% cashback** on Legend platform fees from losing trades. This cashback accrues automatically and can be claimed from the Rewards section in your profile.
