Ranks compares performance across traders, clans, and trading seasons. Open **Ranks** in Legend and choose the leaderboard that matches what you want to compare.

## Traders

The Traders leaderboard supports **24-hour**, **7-day**, **30-day**, and **all-time** views. You can search for a trader and sort the table by fields including profit, average ROI, volume, last activity, Trading ELO, and copiers.

A trader's [Trading ELO](/trading/elo) is an all-time performance rating. It is separate from the selected leaderboard period and from season standings.

## Clans

The Clans leaderboard supports the same 24-hour, 7-day, 30-day, and all-time periods. A clan's canonical rank is based on total PnL. The table can also be sorted by clan name, member count, or total account value without changing that canonical PnL rank.

See [Clans](/clans) for membership rules and volume-based fee rewards.

## Seasons

Season leaderboards use fixed start and end dates and can provide weekly and final standings. Each season defines its own available performance tracks and qualification requirements; tracks may include PnL, ROI, and trading volume.

Season standings are calculated within the selected season or week. They do not reset or replace the all-time Trading ELO shown on a trader's profile.
