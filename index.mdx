Legend is a social trading platform built on Hyperliquid. Trade perps, discover traders, copy positions, share market ideas, and track performance across trader, clan, and season rankings.

## Products

| Product | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| [Web Terminal](https://app.legend.trade) | Full-featured trading terminal with advanced charting, a social feed, and copy trading |
| [iOS App](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/legend-trade-anything/id6754900991) | Trade and follow markets on the go |
| [Android App](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=trade.legend.legend) | The full Legend experience, on Google Play |
| [Telegram Bot](/telegram) | Trade, track positions, and compete from any chat |
| [Copy Trading](/copy-trading) | Mirror positions from top traders automatically or copy an individual trade |
| [Clans](/clans) | Team up, grow together, and unlock shared rewards |
| [Ranks](/ranks) | Compare traders, clans, and season performance |
| [Referrals](/rewards-and-points/referrals-and-rewards) | Earn up to 20% of referred users' fee revenue |
