Legend is a social onchain trading platform. Trade perps across crypto, stocks, and commodities, discover top traders, copy positions, and climb the leaderboard with your clan.

| [Android App](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=trade.legend.legend) | The full Legend experience, on Google Play |
