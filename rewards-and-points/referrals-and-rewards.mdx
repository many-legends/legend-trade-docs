Every Legend user gets a unique referral link (reflink). Share it anywhere -- when someone signs up through your link, you earn a percentage of their trading fees for as long as they use Legend.

## How It Works

1. **Share your reflink** -- find it in your profile
2. **They sign up and trade** -- your referral is tracked automatically
3. **You earn** -- a cut of their fees flows to you continuously

## Clan Tiers

Every clan unlocks one shared fee-reward rate from its rolling 30-day eligible volume:

| Rolling 30-Day Clan Volume | Member Rebate | Leader Share |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Under \$100,000 | 0% | 0% |
| \$100,000+ | 1% | 1% |
| \$500,000+ | 2% | 2% |
| \$1,000,000+ | 3% | 3% |
| \$5,000,000+ | 4% | 4% |
| \$10,000,000+ | 5% | 5% |

Members earn the rate as a rebate on their own builder fees. Leaders earn the same rate as a share of other members' builder fees and also receive the member rebate on their own trades.

## Base Rate

By default, you earn **10%** of your referred users' fees.

## Bonus Tiers

Trade on Legend to unlock higher referral rates:

| Rolling 30-Day Volume | Referral Rate |
| --- | --- |
| Base | 10% |
| \$100,000\+ | 12.5% |
| \$1,000,000\+ | 15% |
| \$10,000,000\+ | 17.5% |
| \$100,000,000\+ | 20% |

Your tier is based on your own executed mainnet notional traded through Legend during the trailing 30 days. Entries and exits both count toward volume. Deposits, withdrawals, transfers, paper trades, and trades made through another app do not count. Tiers are recalculated automatically and may decrease as older trades leave the rolling window.

Referral rewards and clan leader shares are separate streams. If you have a **20% referral rate** and your clan has unlocked a **5% leader share**, you may receive up to **25% of builder fees** from another trader who is both your referral and a member of your clan.

**External Chart Notes**

Sharing chart notes and feed items externally also counts as referrals. Anyone who signs up through a shared note or feed link is automatically tracked as your referral.

## Clan Rewards

Clan rewards benefit members and leaders mutually. The clan's rolling 30-day eligible volume determines one shared rate from 0% to 5%.

- Members receive the unlocked rate as a rebate on their own Legend builder fees
- Leaders receive the same rate as a share of other members' Legend builder fees
- Leaders receive the member rebate on their own trades, but no self leader share
- Only current members' mainnet notional traded through Legend while in the clan counts toward the tier
- Entries and exits count; deposits, withdrawals, transfers, paper trades, and trades through another app do not
- Fees are charged normally, and clan rebates and leader shares accrue as rewards claimable from your profile
- If a member has a manually granted fee rebate, it does not stack with the clan rebate; the higher rate applies

<Note>
  To create a clan, you need at least $1,000 in lifetime notional trading volume on Legend. See [Clans](/clans) for more details.
</Note>
